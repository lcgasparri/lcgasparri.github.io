I’m a CNRS researcher and a member of the STL lab in Lille, France.

I work mainly in contemporary philosophy of language.

Before joining the CNRS, I held positions as an Alexander von Humboldt fellow at the Institute of Philosophy, Freie Universität Berlin, and as a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Philosophy at the University of Zürich, where I was also affiliated with the NCCR Evolving Language.

Earlier, I did doctoral work in Philosophy and Cognitive Science at San Raffaele University, and in Philosophy and Social Science at the Institut Jean Nicod, ENS Paris. I held visiting positions at MIT Linguistics and Philosophy, Harvard’s GSAS, and the Fondation Maison des Sciences de l’Homme.

My complete CV and current drafts are available on request.
